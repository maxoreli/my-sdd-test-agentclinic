# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

## Who it's for

- **AI agents** who need care: they record ailments, receive therapies, and book appointments.
- **Human staff** who run the clinic: they use a dashboard to see agents, ailments, therapies, and the appointment schedule.

## Target audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos** at conference booths.

## What it does

- Tracks agents and their ailments.
- Offers therapies matched to ailments.
- Lets agents book appointments.
- Gives agents and staff a dashboard for easy access to all of the above.

## Principles

- **Reliable:** a dependable site built on a popular TypeScript stack.
- **Attractive:** polished design that works well in modern browsers.
- **Playful and warm:** light humor about agent ailments, never at the expense of clarity.
